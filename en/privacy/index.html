4. Providers and disclosure

Cloudflare provides site delivery and security; Google Workspace provides privacy-channel infrastructure. No transfer of data from these two activities to another third party for that party's own purposes has been identified; any material change would require an update to this notice.

5. Use limitations, withdrawal and ARCO rights

A data subject may request access, rectification, cancellation or objection, and may restrict use or withdraw consent where applicable. The procedure provides for privacy email, in-person delivery and postal delivery to the controller's address. Only the information needed to identify the data subject or representative and locate the data will be requested; the route and receipt must be verified before this notice activates.

The law provides for communicating an ARCO determination within twenty business days of receipt and, where applicable, making it effective within the following fifteen business days, with one justified extension. The deadline control is prepared but has not been tested on a real case; this remains an operational publication blocker.

The website does not retain local per-request HTTP logs. ARCO communications and minimum evidence will be retained only for the time needed to process them, comply with applicable obligations and address responsibilities. Detailed internal retention periods remain a candidate policy: they are not published as a commitment until their execution is checked in the mailbox, private register and backups.

7. Security and incidents

The private register is held outside Git with restricted access. Processing requires minimum identity checks, limited retention and incident assessment without disclosing data through logistics channels. Sustained operation of these controls remains subject to verification.

8. Changes and future activities

Changes to this notice will be communicated at this route with an effective date. This notice does not cover accounts, newsletters, payments, chat, pixels, first-party analytics cookies or forms, none of which are active on the website.
