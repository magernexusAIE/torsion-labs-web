Institutional channel

Institutional contact by email.

For a voluntary collaboration inquiry, write to operacion@torsion-labs.com. Briefly describe the project or question you are interested in and, if relevant, your institution. You do not need to send identification documents, credentials, sensitive data or confidential material for this first contact.

We will use the message data to address your inquiry and provide necessary follow-up within that context, not to subscribe you to newsletters or start campaigns. Read about collaboration inquiries in the privacy notice. Privacy and ARCO rights requests use a separate channel. Inquiries are handled by people, with no promise of an immediate response or continuous monitoring.
