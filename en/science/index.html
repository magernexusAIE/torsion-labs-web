Federated method

Preserving divergence also produces knowledge.

Distinct research agents independently retrieve sources, derive, reproduce code, and seek contradictions. Human adjudication preserves provenance, limits, and direction. Consensus never replaces a reproducible test, a measurement, or competent review.

  1. FormulateDefine question, scope, and refutation criterion.
  2. DeriveSeparate constants, assumptions, and dimensional relations.
  3. ContrastCross sources, code, agents, and negative controls.
  4. ComputeRun local simulations or governed workloads.
  5. ReviewBound which claim–evidence relation a competent, independent person could accept.
  6. ReconcileRecord agreements, divergences, and the next protocol.
