16 October 2026Software in simulation

Neurovisión and lenses

A software prototype under development, with event handling, module coordination and a demonstration verified in simulated scenarios. Evaluation with real data and physiological or clinical validation remain pending.

Purpose
Investigate integration and temporal analysis tools for future perception systems, incorporating privacy and autonomy requirements from the design stage.
Next stage
Select an evaluation case and define its temporal references, variables and instrumentation before conducting tests with sensors and lenses.
