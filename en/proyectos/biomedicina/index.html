What it is & explicit boundaries

OmniPredict investigates how to combine computational modeling and deterministic auditing to study multiscale biological processes reproducibly.

Current stage: R2.1 snapshot running 196/196 passing automated tests, SHA-256 verification, exact arithmetic, and the M2-Regeneración quantitative bone remodeling pilot on auditable public datasets. Clinical evaluation remains out of scope.

Purpose: Build auditable computational tools capable of flagging batch bias, unit mismatches, or tautologies before making biological claims.

Next stage: Finish reconciling resorption/formation matrices in the bone pilot and submit the protocol for independent biophysical review.
