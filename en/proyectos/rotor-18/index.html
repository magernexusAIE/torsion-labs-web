Rotor 18 · Coherence physics

Physics research through mathematical derivation, computational models and simulation, aimed at defining a future experimental comparison.

What it is

Rotor 18 is the computational physics frontier of the Torsión ecosystem: the space where the coherence hypothesis is developed through derivation, dimensional consistency and simulation. Its purpose is to define which predictions can be submitted for review and, under defined measurement conditions, compared with reproducible physical evidence.

The other domains share this discipline of provenance, critical examination and review, without inheriting its assumptions as facts. Each project retains its own questions, conditions of application and validation requirements.

By design, Rotor 18 is the section with the strongest intellectual property protection in the laboratory: we publish the manifestations and their evidence, not the mechanism. Public communication explains what we investigate, which results we can substantiate and what remains open, while safeguarding the research core.

Current stage: A software prototype under development, with module integration and combined result querying verified through reproducible tests using synthetic data. Calibration and comparison with physical measurements remain pending.

Public objective: Establish which proposed physical manifestations can be assessed through reproducible measurements while protecting the central mechanism.

Next stage: Obtain independent technical review of the models’ physical scope and define a measurement protocol covering variables, units, instrumentation and uncertainty.
