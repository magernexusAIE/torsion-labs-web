Multilingual education platform

A layered sovereign educational platform for limited-connectivity contexts, with structural ethics and 22 Mexican languages as a design and future community-validation scope.

What it is

The platform transforms curricular content into multimedia adapted to each community's real substrate: the connectivity available, the declared language, the educational level. Its guiding principle is lossless transformation — content can change form without losing its original pedagogical intention.

The scope includes 22 Mexican languages as first-class citizens of the design. Linguistic and semantic fidelity still requires corpora, community review, and dedicated evaluation; the ethics module already operates as a computational rule for each transformation.

Current stage: an Android client and offline-first engine run locally with a catalog, integrity-preserving storage, and two learning journeys. Content remains a fixture; this does not establish coverage in 22 languages, measured learning, or educational deployment.

Public objective: make scientifically traceable knowledge available in relevant formats and languages without losing pedagogical intent or cultural dignity.

Next verifiable milestone: complete pedagogical, linguistic, and community review of the content before distribution signing, publication, or learning measurement.

Free distribution: this page will become the download point once a distribution identity, signed release build, and authorized content exist. No APK or installation link is offered today, so a laboratory build is not presented as an educational product.

Lab updates

  • Offline-first software
    August 2026

    Two journeys run locally without depending on the Internet

    The Android client loads verified packages, preserves integrity, selects versions, and runs two journeys through the same declarative engine. This local test is not approved content, a distributed APK, community adoption, or measured learning.

  • Verifiable software
    July 2026 · week 1

    Ethics validation went from syntactic to structural

    The governed review detected that the ethics module was verifying word mentions, not properties of the content. It was rebuilt under a new principle: values are inferred from the situation — a public primary-education cycle implies equity and community structurally; the multilingual selection already is the act of inclusion. Curricular alignment moved from a binary flag to a modeled score. Local tests checked the implemented rules; they do not measure learning, cultural fidelity, or community adoption.

Local tests establish software transformations and rules. Language coverage, cultural fidelity, accessibility, and learning remain future validations with community participation. The internal mechanism is never published.
