Software platform in development

SED · Security, Economics and International Law

Technology to expand professional capabilities.

SED is a Torsión software platform conceived to connect information, risk analysis and legal knowledge within environments adaptable to the work of professionals and organizations.

Its development starts from a vision: enabling documents, versions, reference sources and outstanding questions to be connected within one environment, preserving the context needed to investigate, analyze and make decisions.

Professional practice as the starting point

Every organization brings together its own knowledge, procedures and needs. SED proposes building tools around that reality.

For a law firm, this could take the form of an environment for exploring the information associated with a matter, recognizing changes, linking documents to their references and keeping issues requiring professional attention visible.

The scope can address contracts, corporate documentation, intellectual property or other processes defined according to the team’s specialty.

An environment suited to your operations

The architecture of each application is planned around its needs for work, access and control of information.

This approach supports the design of local configurations, with storage and processing within the firm’s infrastructure, for organizations that need to keep client data there. Other configurations can also be explored according to each organization’s circumstances.

Customization covers both functionality and the ways information is organized, accessed and presented.

Artificial intelligence that strengthens human judgment

At Torsión, we believe artificial intelligence can expand people’s ability to understand problems, connect knowledge and develop better tools for their work.

SED brings that approach into professional practice: technology conceived to support specialists, draw on their experience and open new possibilities for analysis, keeping professional judgment and responsibility at the center.

A platform with room to grow

The connection between security, economics and law opens possibilities for developing applications that evolve from a specific task into broader working environments.

Our proposal is for SED to grow with the needs of those who use it, incorporating tools and connections that respond to their practice.

Torsión invites industry to explore this proposal

We invite law firms, specialist teams and organizations to discover SED, examine its possibilities and explore applications of artificial intelligence within their own fields.

Lab updates

  • Security and economic analysis
    October 15, 2026

    Exploring risk through security and economics

    This research area aims to connect digital security, economic analysis and international legal context. We have progressed in tests of Aegis and the economic engine using simulated situations. The project is at the development stage and needs specialist evaluation before real-world use is considered. Current tests do not establish production protection, financial accuracy or legal correctness.

  • Technical update
    August 2026 · week 2

    Aegis remains at the research stage

    Connection with other systems remains subject to evaluation. Local tests do not demonstrate protection of a real network or production use.

  • Verifiable software
    July 2026 · week 1

    Risk normalization checked in simulated scenarios

    The local technical review confirmed that the engine applies a maximum —not average— criterion to regulatory tension, bounded its historical memory, and removed dead code. The tests verify these software rules; they do not establish defense of a public network, regulatory compliance, or financial accuracy in production.

Every update separates local software behavior, simulated scenarios, and future operational objectives. Research preserves the limits among security, economics, and law.

We present the architecture and the evidence, not the core.
